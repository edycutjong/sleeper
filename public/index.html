On 2024-03-29 a backdoor was found in xz-utils (CVE-2024-3094), planted over roughly two years by a contributor who built trust one innocuous commit at a time. No single commit was suspicious. It was caught five weeks after the poisoned release shipped, because one engineer investigated 500 ms of unexplained SSH login latency.

Press replay. The real public timeline streams into CockroachDB event by event, the agent rolls it into a behavioural arc, searches its own memory, and decides whether to let the release out.

Running with the offline stand-in for AWS Bedrock — wiring only. Similarity numbers on this page are from a hash function, not a model.
